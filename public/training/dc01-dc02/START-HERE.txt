1. Extract this entire folder from the ZIP.
2. Open index.html in a current Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari browser.
3. Choose a chapter. The 3D network follows your place in the lesson.
4. Try Security reasoning and My evidence. Enter observations, check the
   three items, answer the question, and mark the chapter complete.
5. Export your record before changing browsers or devices. Import that JSON
   record to resume in a different browser. You can also copy the record
   from its preview and paste it into Import record. Readable notes export
   as text.

WORKBOOK AND NOTICES
The 14-page workbook includes foundations (including the ports a domain
member needs), configuration steps, verification prompts with an optional
Packet Monitor capture, evidence questions, and Notices and permissions on
page 14. QR codes open the matching steps in Inside the Network.
The table of contents links directly to each section. The guide footer links
to notices.html, which includes the rights notice and software license link.

The lesson code and 3D assets are bundled without external dependencies. The workbook links
need the files to stay beside index.html. Journal articles and source links
need an internet connection. Some mobile file viewers restrict local HTML;
open it in a full browser or use the published guide on securebydesigninc.com.

This proof of concept contains sample values and source-checked lesson
content. It has not been validated on your Windows Server environment. Your recorded
video, original screenshots, and timestamps are not included yet. The video
area intentionally describes the upcoming recording rather than pretending
to play a video. The app view is a responsive browser presentation, not an
installed native app. Progress is local; no account or cloud sync is used.

The scene illustrates chapter states. It does not connect to your VMs, run
commands, or validate your server configuration. Completion records your
learning activity. Keep credentials out of notes and evidence exports.

WEBSITE AND APP DELIVERY
This package demonstrates the lesson content and interactions. A published
edition can connect the video chapters, shared lesson content, and network
states to the website and app, then use the account service for progress.
Each episode can connect viewers to the same companion and its chapters.
