Secure By Design - ATO Template Library (DoD SAP / JSIG edition)
Prepared 15 October 2026 for instruction and practice. Not official forms.

EDITION
These templates follow the DoD Joint SAP Implementation Guide (JSIG) of 11 April 2016
with the errata of 5 October 2018, which is built on NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 4 and the
2014 CNSSI 1253 baselines. No Rev. 5 JSIG or JSIG successor had been published as of
October 2026. DoDM 5205.07 (17 January 2025) requires the JSIG or its successor.
Your authorizing official's and PSO's current templates always take precedence.
