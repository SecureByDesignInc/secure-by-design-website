Run each check in an elevated PowerShell session, or a shell on the Linux servers, on the host the lesson names.
Some lessons include the configuration commands they verify: read each command before you run it, and adapt
the example names, addresses, and paths to your own plan. Nothing in this file runs by itself.

1. Design the rings and the software rules
# Capture the before state: where machines currently look for updates (nowhere internal yet).
Get-ItemProperty 'HKLM:\SOFTWARE\Policies\Microsoft\Windows\WindowsUpdate' -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue
Get-ADComputer -Filter * -SearchBase 'OU=SBD,DC=sbd,DC=test' | Measure-Object

What success looks like: The ring table names every system's ring, the one-source rule and feeding cadence are written, and the register carries the course's rows with owners.

2. Build SBD-WSUS01 and the ring policies
Get-WsusServer | Get-WsusComputer -ComputerTargetGroups 'Ring 1 - Pilot' | Select-Object FullDomainName, LastReportedStatusTime
gpresult /r /scope:computer
Get-WindowsUpdateLog -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue

What success looks like: Every system reports to its ring's group, the pilot ring installed and reported the first approved cycle, and the promotion to later rings followed the written order.

3. Build SBD-REPO01 for approved packages
Test-NetConnection 10.0.70.20 -Port 80
# On a Linux consumer: confirm the only configured source is the internal repository
# dnf repolist  (or the platform equivalent) — capture the output into the evidence set
Get-FileHash -Algorithm SHA256 C:\Evidence\patch-protect\repo-snapshot-catalog.txt

What success looks like: The repository answers on its registered path, consumers list the internal source and only the internal source, and the snapshot catalog with hashes is filed.

4. SBD-MGMT01 and the living inventory
Get-ADComputer -Filter * -SearchBase 'OU=SBD,DC=sbd,DC=test' | Measure-Object
Get-DhcpServerv4Lease -ScopeId 10.0.80.0 | Measure-Object
# Compare both counts against the inventory's totals and record the reconciliation

What success looks like: The inventory lists every modeled system with its attributes, the first directory-and-DHCP reconciliation matches (or its mismatches are findings), and ownership and cadence are written.

5. Endpoint protection through SBD-EPO01
Test-NetConnection SBD-EPO01.sbd.test -Port 443
Get-Service -ComputerName SBD-PC01 -Name 'masvc' -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue | Select-Object Status
Get-WinEvent -LogName ForwardedEvents -MaxEvents 5 | Select-Object TimeCreated, MachineName

What success looks like: Every inventoried endpoint shows a checking-in agent, the policy baseline is applied and recorded, and a test detection traveled from endpoint to console to the analyst's search.

6. SBD-NESSUS01 and the standing cadence
Test-NetConnection SBD-NESSUS01.sbd.test -Port 8834
Get-ChildItem C:\Evidence\patch-protect -Recurse | Select-Object Name, LastWriteTime
Get-FileHash -Algorithm SHA256 C:\Evidence\patch-protect\*

What success looks like: The credentialed scan covered the inventory with the scan account's use logged, every finding is classified, results and schedule are wired into pipeline and cadence, and the course's baselines and rescans are filed.
