YOUR LEARNING PATH

LESSON 1 OF 6 · ABOUT 20 MIN

Design and build the OU structure

Organizational units exist for delegation and Group Policy targeting, not to mirror an org chart. Build the structure the hardening policies will hang on before importing any of them.

In this lesson you will

  • Design an OU structure around delegation and Group Policy targeting.
  • Create protected OUs and redirect the default Computers and Users containers.
  • Explain why the built-in Domain Controllers OU stays untouched.

Work through the configuration

  1. Design the structure by administration: a top-level SBD OU containing Servers, Workstations, Users, Groups, and an Admin OU for privileged accounts. The built-in Domain Controllers OU stays where it is and keeps its name.
  2. Create the planned OUs with accidental-deletion protection enabled. Record each OU's purpose and who will administer its contents; an OU with no delegation or policy purpose is clutter.
  3. Redirect the default Computers and Users containers to managed OUs with redircmp and redirusr. Group Policy cannot be linked to the default containers, so anything landing there would get only domain-level policy, never the OU-linked hardening.
  4. Keep privileged accounts and systems separate: the Admin OU holds the identities that administer domain controllers. The issuing CA from course 1.4 joins the Servers OU for now; course 1.5 gives Tier 0 servers their own OU. Do not nest deeper than the delegation plan requires.

Inspect your configuration

Run the relevant checks in an elevated PowerShell session — or a shell, on the Linux servers — on the host identified in the lesson. Adapt the example names and paths to your lab.

Get-ADOrganizationalUnit -Filter * | Select-Object Name, DistinguishedName
Get-ADDomain | Select-Object ComputersContainer, UsersContainer
(Get-ADOrganizationalUnit -Identity 'OU=SBD,DC=sbd,DC=test' -Properties ProtectedFromAccidentalDeletion).ProtectedFromAccidentalDeletion

What success looks like

The planned OUs exist with deletion protection, the default container redirects point at managed OUs, and each OU has a recorded purpose and administrator.

Keep your evidence

Keep the OU design sheet: each OU's distinguished name, purpose, delegated administrator, and the redirect commands with their output.
