The Guided portfolio track · coming soon

Four phases, each ending in work you can show an employer

You build, secure and document a complete network, the way a security team would. A written review every other week, with peer review in between, keeps each piece at a standard a hiring manager will take seriously.

  1. Phase 1 · Weeks 1–4Build

    Active Directory, DNS and a two-tier PKI, with network diagrams and build documentation.

  2. Phase 2 · Weeks 5–8Harden

    A Group Policy baseline, STIG and SCAP scan results, and a remediation report.

  3. Phase 3 · Weeks 9–11Authorize

    An RMF package: System Security Plan, POA&M and control evidence.

  4. Phase 4 · Week 12Capstone

    A recorded authorization briefing and a portfolio page you can send to employers.

You leave with a public portfolio of write-ups and diagrams, a Secure By Design certificate of completion (a record of the track you finished, not a professional certification), and a reference based on the work we reviewed together.
