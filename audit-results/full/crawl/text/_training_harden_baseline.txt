Build the structure the hardening policies will hang on before importing any of them.
In this lesson you willDesign an OU structure around delegation and Group Policy targeting.Create protected OUs and redirect the default Computers and Users containers.Explain why the built-in Domain Controllers OU stays untouched.Work through the configurationDesign the structure by administration: a top-level SBD OU containing Servers, Workstations, Users, Groups, and an Admin OU for privileged accounts.
The built-in Domain Controllers OU stays where it is and keeps its name.Create the planned OUs with accidental-deletion protection enabled.
Record each OU's purpose and who will administer its contents; an OU with no delegation or policy purpose is clutter.Redirect the default Computers and Users containers to managed OUs with redircmp and redirusr.
Group Policy cannot be linked to the default containers, so anything landing there would join the domain unmanaged.Keep privileged accounts and systems separate: the Admin OU holds the identities that administer domain controllers, and the next course places the issuing CA in the Servers tier.
Do not nest deeper than the delegation plan requires.Inspect your configurationRun the relevant checks in an elevated PowerShell session on the host identified in the lesson.
